What the app does

FaceScan analyzes a photo you provide to estimate face shape, jaw angle, facial symmetry, and facial thirds, using an open-source facial landmark model that runs entirely in your browser. It then suggests hairstyles based on those measurements, and offers an optional 30-day facial exercise plan.

Not medical or professional advice

Measurements are approximate and sensitive to photo angle, lighting, and head position. Nothing in the app is a medical diagnosis, a clinical assessment, or a rating of attractiveness, and it should not be relied on as such. The facial exercise plan consists of general muscle-toning exercises ("face yoga") — it does not reshape bone, is not a medical or cosmetic treatment, and you should stop any exercise that causes pain.
