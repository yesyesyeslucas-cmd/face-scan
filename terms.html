What the app does

FaceScan analyzes a photo you provide to estimate face shape, jaw angle, facial symmetry, and facial thirds, using an open-source facial landmark model that runs entirely in your browser. It then suggests hairstyles based on those measurements, and offers an optional 90-day facial exercise plan.

Free features and Premium subscription

The front-facing face shape scan, your first hairstyle recommendation, and the first 7 days of the exercise plan are free for everyone, with no account or payment required. The following features require an active Premium subscription:

  • Side profile analysis (nasal bridge angle, chin projection, jaw profile)
  • The complete hairstyle guide (all recommendations matched to your face shape)
  • The full 90-day facial exercise plan (days 8 through 90)
