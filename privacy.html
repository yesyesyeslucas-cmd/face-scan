Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information FaceScan ("the app") uses and how. FaceScan is a face-shape analysis and hairstyle recommendation app that runs entirely in your device's browser.

Photos you upload

When you upload a front-facing or side-profile photo to scan, it is processed entirely on your own device, in your browser, using an on-device facial landmark model (face-api.js). The photo is never uploaded to, stored on, or seen by any server operated by the app. Closing the app, restarting a scan, or navigating away clears it from memory.

Google Sign-In

If you choose "Sign in with Google," authentication is handled directly by Google's own sign-in system. The app receives only your name, email address, and profile picture, as authorized by you through Google's account chooser, to personalize your session. The app never sees or stores your Google password. This information is kept only in your browser's local storage on your own device and is not transmitted to any server operated by the app. You may also use the app as a Guest without signing in at all.

Gender and date of birth

During first-time setup, the app asks for your gender and date of birth to personalize hairstyle suggestions. This information is stored only in your device's local browser storage, is never sent to a server, and is not linked to your Google account.

30-day exercise plan progress

If you use the optional facial exercise plan, which days you've marked complete are stored only in your device's local browser storage. This progress is not synced to any account or server, so it will not carry over if you switch devices, reinstall the app, or clear your browser data.

Language preference

Your selected display language is stored only in your device's local browser storage so the app remembers it next time.

Third parties

The app loads scripts from their official sources: face-api.js (an open-source facial landmark library) and Google Identity Services (for sign-in). These providers may see standard technical request data (such as your IP address) as an ordinary function of loading a script from the internet, governed by their own privacy policies.

No advertising or tracking

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics trackers, or third-party cookies, and does not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Your choices

You can use the app without signing in at all via "Continue as Guest." You can clear your stored sign-in at any time using the "switch" option in the app, or by clearing your browser's site data for this page — this also removes any locally stored gender, date of birth, and exercise progress.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time; the "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent change.

Contact
